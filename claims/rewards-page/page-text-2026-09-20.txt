FAQ
What is Rakeback?

Rakeback rewards you for your bets in real time, calculated per bet rather than as a flat percentage of your total wagering.

How is Rakeback calculated?

Rakeback is based on multiple factors like your bet size, game RTP, and house edge, with each bet contributing individually to your total rewards.

How do I activate the 15% Instant Rakeback?

Head to the Rewards page, open "Special Rewards," and enter an affiliate code to activate your 7-day boost.

Why can't I see my Weekly and Monthly rewards?

Your rewards are still building in the background and will only appear once they're ready to be claimed at the end of the period.

What types of rewards are available?

You can earn Instant, Weekly, and Monthly rewards, all claimable via the Rewards page.

When can I claim Instant Rakeback?

Instant Rakeback becomes claimable as soon as you reach the minimum amount, with no expiry once unlocked.

Do rewards expire?

Instant rewards don't expire once unlocked. Weekly and Monthly rewards must be claimed within their respective timeframes.

What is the Rewards System?

It's a dynamic system that offers personalised bonuses, rank progression, and multiple reward types based on your activity and gameplay.

Do I earn Rakeback while using a bonus?

No. Rakeback isn't generated while you're completing wager requirements from deposit bonuses.

Where do I claim my rewards?

All rewards can be claimed directly from the Rewards page once they become available.

Welcome to Gamdom Rewards

Welcome to Gamdom Rewards

At Gamdom, every bet you place earns you something back. Our Rewards program is designed to reward you consistently — through Rakeback, Cashback, and a progression system that gets more valuable the more you play.

Rakeback & Cashback, working together

Rakeback returns a percentage of your wagers back to you as you play, while Cashback covers a portion of your losses over time. Together, they make sure you're always getting value from your time on the platform, regardless of how a session goes.

Progress through the tiers

As your activity grows, so do your rewards. Moving up through Gamdom's reward tiers unlocks better Rakeback rates, higher Cashback percentages, and a 25% rewards boost — giving you more reason to keep playing and more value at every level.

Personalised to your play

Your rewards are calculated based on your own wagering history, so what you earn reflects how you actually play. No one-size-fits-all bonuses — just consistent, fair rewards tailored to you.

Track and redeem anytime

Your Rakeback balance and Cashback earnings are always visible on the Rewards page. Redeem when it suits you and keep track of your progress toward the next tier — all in one place.
